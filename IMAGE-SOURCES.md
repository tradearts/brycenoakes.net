# Image sources

All images are local and sourced from Bryce Noakes / Trade Arts project records.
No stock or AI-generated imagery is used. The Flybuys cover is a frame from the
publicly released campaign and is identified below.

## Art direction rules

- Colour photography carries place, movement and finished work.
- Monochrome is used for the portrait and close process details as a deliberate
  field-note register.
- Images include intrinsic dimensions and are kept within the useful limits of
  their source material. Responsive variants are used for the large workshop image.
