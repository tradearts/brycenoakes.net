# Image sources

All images are local and sourced from Bryce Noakes / Trade Arts project records.
No stock, AI-generated or video-frame imagery is used.

## Art direction rules

- Colour photography carries place, person and finished work.
- Monochrome is reserved for the close process details in the `detail-strip`, so
  the mixed source treatment reads as a deliberate register rather than an accident.
- No image is displayed wider than its source supports at a 2× device pixel ratio.
  Where a source has a hard ceiling (the reVITALise renders at 1424 px, the portrait
  at 680 px), the layout caps the display size rather than upscaling.
