Public art development · 2026

reVITALise

Turning artist Belem Lett’s looping visual language into a coordinated, buildable system for the Park Avenue pedestrian walkway in Coffs Harbour.

Artist
Belem Lett
Trade Arts role
Concept development, design coordination, technical translation and fabrication strategy
Status
Commissioned Stage 1 completed and delivered. Construction was subsequently deferred by the City.

From artistic gesture to physical system.

The challenge was to preserve the character of the artwork while resolving a real public site with walls, stairs, overhead structure and pedestrian movement.

Trade Arts developed the preferred direction into a modular family of forms, tested how it would move across the walkway, and established an approach to fabrication, fixing, finishing and future technical documentation.

The outcome was a clear Stage 1 package that connected artistic intent, spatial relationships and buildability. The project remains unbuilt.
