Public art development · 2026

reVITALise

Leading a council-procured Stage 1 commission that translated artist Belem Lett’s visual language into a coordinated, reviewable package for a complex public site.

Artist
Belem Lett
Client
City of Coffs Harbour
Brief
Carry the artist’s looping visual language through the Park Avenue pedestrian walkway
Responsibilities
Procurement response, artist and stakeholder coordination, scope and budget management, concept development, 3D design, visualisation and fabrication strategy
Constraints
Existing walls, stairs, overhead structure and pedestrian movement
Outcome
Commissioned Stage 1 completed and delivered. Construction was subsequently deferred by the City; the project remains unbuilt.

From artistic gesture to physical system.

Trade Arts won the commission through a council procurement process. I led the interface between Belem, the City, the design team and consultants while holding scope, budget and constructability around the artist’s intent.

We developed the preferred direction into a modular family of forms, tested how it would move across the walkway, and established preliminary approaches to fabrication, finishing, installation and later technical resolution.

The outcome was a clear Stage 1 package that connected artistic intent, spatial relationships and buildability. The project remains unbuilt.
