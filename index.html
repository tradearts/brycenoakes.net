Profile

I work where creative intent meets practical delivery.

My background crosses visual arts, photography, film and television, spatial design and specialist fabrication.

Since founding Trade Arts in 2024, I have led projects from briefing and design development through estimating, procurement, production and handover. I’m most useful when a strong idea needs structure, judgement and forward motion.

I coordinate the people a project actually needs — artists, designers, CAD specialists, engineers, suppliers and installers — and stay accountable for the result that arrives on site.

Workshop & mobile production

A deployable build system, not just a design service.

Production resources available through Trade Arts include approximately 10 professional FDM printers, five resin printers, CNC, compressors, spray booths, scenic equipment and comprehensive workshop tools.

Mobile logistics include a BYD 4WD ute with a 2.5-tonne towing rating and a large box trailer. Equipment and transport are subject to project scope, location and commercial arrangement.
