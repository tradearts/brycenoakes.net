01 Position

I work in the gap between a strong idea and the thing that actually arrives.

My job is to give creative ambition structure — a clear brief, an honest scope, the right people, workable decisions and enough forward motion to get it over the line.

I can talk with an artist in the morning, solve a fabrication problem after lunch and still keep an eye on budget, programme and handover.
