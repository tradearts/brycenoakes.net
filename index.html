02 / Profile

Creative enough to see the opportunity. Practical enough to make it real.

My background crosses visual arts, photography, screen production, set design and specialist fabrication. That range has made me a generalist in the useful sense: I can understand the intent, identify the constraint and organise the people and decisions needed to move forward.
